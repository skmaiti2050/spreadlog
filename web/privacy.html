Spreadlog Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 | Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Spreadlog ("we", "us", or "our") is a privacy-first, local-first electronic aviation logbook designed for pilots. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled across our mobile application (Android) and web application (https://spreadlog.incognitos.workers.dev).

1. Local-First Philosophy & No Tracking

Spreadlog is architected around local-first data ownership. All your flight logs, aircraft entries, pilot profile attributes, and scanned endorsement certificates are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database.

  • We do not operate tracking servers, advertising networks, or third-party behavioral telemetry.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your personal flight data or identity.
  • Your data belongs exclusively to you.

2. Google Account Integration & OAuth Scopes

Spreadlog offers optional cloud synchronization and backup dispatch via Google APIs. To adhere to the principle of least privilege, Spreadlog strictly requests the absolute minimum OAuth scopes required:

  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Allows Spreadlog to create and access only the specific files and folders that the app itself creates (e.g., the Spreadlog Aviation Backups folder and encrypted .spbackup archives). Spreadlog cannot read, view, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Gmail Dispatch (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send.self): Allows Spreadlog to send automated backup archives exclusively to your own authenticated Google email address. Spreadlog cannot read your emails, search your inbox, modify drafts, or send messages to unauthorized third parties.
Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
Spreadlog's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Client-Side Cryptography & Security

Before any backup archive is uploaded to Google Drive or dispatched via Gmail, the archive is encrypted client-side on your device using AES-256-GCM encryption with keys derived via PBKDF2/Argon2 from your user-defined master passphrase.

Neither Google nor any network intermediary possesses the encryption key. Even while resting in cloud storage, your flight logs remain inaccessible without your private master passphrase.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can purge all logbook data at any time directly through the app settings or by uninstalling the application.
  • Cloud Backups: You retain complete autonomy over your Google Drive backups. You may delete individual backup archives or remove the Spreadlog Aviation Backups folder at any time from your Google Drive account.
  • Revoking Access: You can disconnect your Google account from Spreadlog at any time within the app's settings, or centrally via Google Account Security Permissions.

5. Third-Party Services

Aside from Google Drive and Gmail APIs used strictly for user-initiated cloud backups, Spreadlog relies only on standard device operating system services (such as the Google Play Services ML Kit for on-device document scanning). No external third-party SDKs receive your flight log records.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Spreadlog constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
